MSP Tracker

Double-click MSP Tracker to start it. It opens in its own window, and stops when you close the window.

Your information is saved in the Data folder, which appears here after the first run.
To back up, copy the whole MSP Tracker folder. To remove it, delete the folder.

The first time you open it, your computer will warn you that the app isn't from a known
developer. The steps to get past that are on the download page:
https://github.com/invisi101/MSPtracker#install
