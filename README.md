# MSP Tracker

A tracker for the training and nutrition plan your coach gives you. Enter your coach's daily
numbers and workouts, then log your weight, food and training against them. It runs entirely on
your own computer, and everything it saves stays in one folder.

The AI assistant, AI chef and AI food estimates are optional and use your own Anthropic API key,
which you can add in the app. Without a key, everything else works.

## Install

### Mac

1. Download **MSP-Tracker-mac.zip** from the [latest release](https://github.com/invisi101/MSPtracker/releases/latest).
   It works on both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
2. Double-click the zip to unzip it. Drag the **MSP Tracker** folder that appears into **Documents**
   (or anywhere you like, other than Downloads).
3. Open the MSP Tracker folder and double-click **MSP Tracker**.

The first time, macOS blocks it because the app isn't from an identified developer:

- **macOS 15 (Sequoia) or later:** click **Done** on the warning. Open **System Settings →
  Privacy & Security**, scroll down to the message about MSP Tracker, click **Open Anyway**, and confirm.
- **macOS 14 or earlier:** right-click **MSP Tracker**, choose **Open**, then click **Open** again.

After that it opens normally with a double-click.

### Windows

1. Download **MSP-Tracker-windows.zip** from the [latest release](https://github.com/invisi101/MSPtracker/releases/latest).
2. Right-click the zip and choose **Extract All**. Move the **MSP Tracker** folder somewhere you like,
   such as Documents.
3. Open the MSP Tracker folder and double-click **MSP Tracker**.

The first time, Windows shows **"Windows protected your PC"** because the app isn't from a known
publisher. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. After that it opens normally.

## Using it

MSP Tracker opens in its own window and stops when you close the window. Everything you enter is
saved as you go, in the **Data** folder inside the MSP Tracker folder.

- **Back up:** copy the whole MSP Tracker folder.
- **Uninstall:** delete the MSP Tracker folder. Nothing else is left on your computer.
- **Update:** download the new release, then move the **Data** folder from your old MSP Tracker
  folder into the new one.
